# Teaching Depth and Authentic Tool Practice

Use this reference when creating or substantially revising a self-study lesson, especially when the learner must reason through a process or use a named tool.

## Establish prerequisites before procedures

- Identify the learner's assumed knowledge. If essential background is unclear, ask when needed; otherwise introduce the foundation before relying on it.
- Define unfamiliar terms, acronyms and settings in plain language before a formula, instruction or procedure uses them. Ground each in a concrete example or visual; avoid definitions that introduce a stack of unexplained terms.
- Make the connections explicit: explain the purpose of a setting, what it changes, and how the setting affects the example before asking the learner to operate it.

## Teach the reasoning, not a quota

- Preserve relevant source detail. Explain **why**, show a derivation or mental model, then work an example through intermediate values and decisions. State inputs, units, timing and assumptions where they affect the answer.
- Follow with guided and faded practice, independent transfer, and feedback that explains plausible mistakes. Give the exact task's model after the learner attempts it.
- Use as many meaningful blocks or pages as the concept needs. A long paragraph split into many thin pages, or repeated text + callout + one-choice patterns, does not create instructional depth. A block count, page count or automated score is not evidence of learning or rendering quality.
- A distinctive teacher persona can make the explanation engaging; it must not replace the explanation or shorten the mathematical or procedural reasoning.
- Example: before a calculator exercise, explain a period using dates and a concrete rate; show the cash-flow timing and variable mapping; then let the learner operate the relevant keys. A wall of undefined acronyms followed by a decorative button is not a substitute.

## Make named-tool practice authentic

- When the goal is learning a real named calculator, application or device, consult an authoritative reference for the relevant version. Reproduce the relevant control labels and positions and have learners operate those controls. Show the actual state changes and meaningful error recovery.
- Generic Next slides or a table of values are not sufficient when the learner needs to acquire the real interface operation. A full emulator is not automatically required: implement the needed subset faithfully, identify the supported scope, and clearly mark unused or unavailable controls.
- Make wrong actions recoverable and prevent them from silently advancing. If a simulator claims to calculate, its result must come from its supported inputs and state rather than a predetermined output.
- In a TVM calculator example, distinguish rate units and percentage entry from decimal formulas, match rates to periods, show cash-flow signs, explain manual versus automatic conversion settings, and distinguish a final lump sum from recurring PMT where both exist. These are example-specific checks, not mandatory topics in unrelated lessons.

## Visual reasoning and review

- Use a timeline, transformation, worked ledger or other meaningful visual to show the reasoning. A native table may be better than a decorative widget. A simulation must behave as its copy claims, not merely switch text.
- For a large lesson or course, review representative learner-facing copy and the rendered practice experience before scaling, consistent with the applicable scope and approval gates. If the learner rejects an exemplar as shallow, re-audit prerequisites, reasoning, examples and practice; do not merely add headings or pad counts.
- Verify saved player behavior and learning order on desktop and mobile, including feedback, answer gates and recovery paths. A quality score or successful save does not prove pedagogy or rendered behavior.
- When artifacts are used, run their contract validator and exercise all meaningful states: actions, wrong answers, predictions and settings. Check light/dark themes, 320/343/620-pixel portrait frames and adaptive sizing when those host frames apply, plus a reachable completion bridge. Confirm that the test harness traverses the actual states it claims to check.
